Hook up a YouTube playlist

yt-summary monitors any playlist you point at and summarizes new videos as they're added. The killer pattern is your own private "watch later" queue — but you can also subscribe to a public playlist if you just want everything from a channel.

Recommended

Your personal queue

Create an unlisted playlist on YouTube. Whenever you spot a long video while scrolling — on your phone, on the couch, doesn't matter — hit ⋯ → Save to playlist and pick your playlist. Your summary is ready in minutes, here in yt-summary. Skim it, decide whether the full video is worth your time.

One-time setup on YouTube
  1. Open YouTube → Library → Playlists and click + New playlist.
  2. Name it something like yt-summary queue.
  3. Set privacy to Unlisted. (Private won't work — yt-dlp can't see the videos. Unlisted lets yt-summary in via the link without making the playlist searchable.)
  4. Save the empty playlist, then copy its URL from the address bar.
  5. Paste it below. From now on, ⋯ → Save to playlist → your playlist on any video sends it to yt-summary.
