Add a playlist

Subscribe to a YouTube playlist. The newest videos get summarized automatically, and the scheduler re-checks for new entries every hour. Older videos can be loaded later from the playlist page.

How do I find a playlist URL?
  1. On YouTube, open any playlist page.
  2. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar.
  3. It should contain list=PL… or list=UU….

Channel-uploads "playlists" work too — you can grab them from a channel's Videos tab, then click View as playlist.
